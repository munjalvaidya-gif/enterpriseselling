# Objection Bank

Organized by deal stage, since realistic objections change shape as a deal progresses. Each entry: the objection, the MEDDPICC element it's really testing (see `meddpicc-primer.md`), and which persona(s) most naturally raise it. Use this as a pull-from source for the battlecard (Step 2) and as improvisation material during the roleplay (Step 3) — don't reuse the wording verbatim every time; adapt phrasing to the specific account and product context given.

## Discovery stage

Buyer is still deciding if this is worth their time. Objections here are mostly about relevance and priority, not price or terms.

| Objection | MEDDPICC element | Typical persona |
|---|---|---|
| "We're honestly fine with what we have right now." | Identify Pain | End user, Technical Champion |
| "I'm not the right person for this — you'd want to talk to someone else." | Economic Buyer | Any |
| "We looked at something like this a while back and it didn't go anywhere." | Identify Pain / Competition | Economic Buyer, Champion |
| "This isn't really a priority for us this quarter." | Metrics | Economic Buyer |
| "How is this different from what [category] tools already do?" | Decision Criteria | Technical Champion |
| "I don't have a lot of time for this right now." | Identify Pain | Any |
| "Who else have you sold this to that's like us?" | Decision Criteria | Economic Buyer, Champion |

## Evaluation stage

Buyer is actively comparing options. Objections shift toward proof, fit, and internal alignment.

| Objection | MEDDPICC element | Typical persona |
|---|---|---|
| "We're also looking at [unnamed competitor/alternative]." | Competition | Economic Buyer, Procurement |
| "I'd need to run this by my team before I could say yes to anything." | Champion / Economic Buyer | Technical Champion, End user |
| "How do we actually measure whether this is working once it's in?" | Metrics | Economic Buyer |
| "What happens if this doesn't integrate cleanly with [specific system]?" | Decision Criteria | Technical Champion |
| "I've been burned by a vendor overpromising before." | Identify Pain / Decision Criteria | Technical Champion, End user |
| "This feels like a bigger change than we're ready for right now." | Identify Pain | End user |
| "Can you get us in front of a real customer reference?" | Decision Criteria | Economic Buyer, Champion |
| "Our security team will need to weigh in before this goes further." | Paper Process | Technical Champion, Security/Legal |

## Negotiation stage

Buyer is serious. Objections here are sharper and often test whether the seller will fold under pressure.

| Objection | MEDDPICC element | Typical persona |
|---|---|---|
| "This price doesn't work for us — you'll need to do better." | Metrics / Decision Criteria | Procurement |
| "We can get a similar tool for less elsewhere." | Competition | Procurement, Economic Buyer |
| "I still need sign-off from someone above me on this." | Economic Buyer | Champion |
| "We need a shorter contract term before we'll commit." | Decision Process | Procurement |
| "What happens if this doesn't deliver what you're claiming?" | Metrics | Economic Buyer |
| "Our legal team flagged a few things in the contract." | Paper Process | Security/Legal, Procurement |
| "We're not comfortable being the first account like us on this." | Identify Pain / Decision Criteria | Economic Buyer |

## Procurement / paper stage

Deal is effectively decided in principle; objections here are about terms, risk, and process — not whether to buy.

| Objection | MEDDPICC element | Typical persona |
|---|---|---|
| "Security review is going to take longer than you'd like." | Paper Process | Security/Legal |
| "Our standard terms don't allow for [specific clause]." | Paper Process | Procurement, Security/Legal |
| "We need this to go through our full procurement process, no shortcuts." | Decision Process | Procurement |
| "There's a competing internal priority for budget this cycle." | Economic Buyer / Metrics | Economic Buyer |
| "We want a pilot/trial period before the full commitment." | Decision Process | Economic Buyer, Procurement |
| "Data residency/compliance requirements aren't fully addressed yet." | Paper Process | Security/Legal |

## Cross-stage: the compound objection

For adaptive escalation (SKILL.md Step 3) when the seller is handling single objections well, combine two of the above into one exchange rather than pulling a new single objection — e.g. price (Metrics) stacked with a competitor comparison (Competition) in the same breath: *"Even setting price aside, [alternative] already does most of this and we know that team — why would we take on the risk of switching?"* This tests whether the seller can prioritize which thread to answer first rather than getting pulled into answering both shallowly.
