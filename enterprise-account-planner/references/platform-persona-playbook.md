# Platform → Persona & Entry Point Playbook

This maps what the seller sells to who to target and why now. Match the seller's stated platform to the closest category below. If nothing matches well, use the fallback method at the bottom rather than skipping Section 6.

For each category: **Economic Buyer** (who signs), **Champion** (who advocates day-to-day), **Common entry-point triggers** (events that make outreach timely), **Opening angle** (how to frame it).

## CRM / Sales Engagement (e.g. Salesforce, HubSpot, Outreach)
- **Economic Buyer:** CRO, VP Sales Ops, CMO (if marketing-attached)
- **Champion:** Sales Ops Manager, RevOps lead
- **Triggers:** new CRO/CMO hire, sales reorg, missed revenue targets on an earnings call, M&A requiring go-to-market consolidation
- **Opening angle:** tie to the specific revenue or forecasting pain a new leader is publicly accountable for fixing

## Data Platform / Analytics / Cloud Warehouse (e.g. Snowflake, Databricks, BigQuery)
- **Economic Buyer:** CDO, CTO, VP Data/Analytics Engineering
- **Champion:** Head of Data Engineering, Analytics Engineering Manager
- **Triggers:** new CDO/Chief AI Officer hire, public AI initiative announcement, cloud cost-optimization mandate, competitor data breach/outage prompting a platform review
- **Opening angle:** lead with the specific AI/analytics initiative they've announced publicly and the data foundation it requires

## Cloud Infrastructure / DevOps (e.g. AWS, HashiCorp, GitLab)
- **Economic Buyer:** CTO, VP Engineering, VP Infrastructure
- **Champion:** Platform Engineering Lead, SRE Manager
- **Triggers:** cloud migration announcement, engineering headcount growth, publicized outage/incident, datacenter exit
- **Opening angle:** reference the specific migration or reliability initiative and the operational risk it currently carries

## Observability / Monitoring (e.g. Datadog, New Relic, Splunk)
- **Economic Buyer:** VP Engineering, CTO, VP SRE
- **Champion:** SRE Lead, DevOps Manager
- **Triggers:** publicized outage, rapid infrastructure scaling, new SRE/reliability leadership hire
- **Opening angle:** connect to a specific reliability commitment made publicly (earnings call SLA mention, post-incident report)

## Cybersecurity (e.g. CrowdStrike, Okta, Palo Alto Networks)
- **Economic Buyer:** CISO, CIO (in orgs without a dedicated CISO)
- **Champion:** Security Engineering Manager, IT Security Lead
- **Triggers:** new CISO hire, disclosed breach (theirs or a close competitor's), new compliance mandate in their vertical, M&A expanding attack surface
- **Opening angle:** anchor to the regulatory or incident-driven urgency, never generic fear-based pitching

## Fintech / Payments (e.g. Stripe, Adyen, Plaid)
- **Economic Buyer:** CFO, VP Payments/Treasury, Head of Product (for embedded finance)
- **Champion:** Payments Engineering Lead, Product Manager for checkout/billing
- **Triggers:** international expansion announcement, new product line launch, payment-processor outage or dispute rate issue reported publicly
- **Opening angle:** tie to the specific expansion or product launch and the payments complexity it introduces

## HR Tech / Workforce (e.g. Workday, Rippling, Gusto)
- **Economic Buyer:** CHRO, VP People, CFO (for payroll/finance-adjacent tools)
- **Champion:** HRIS Manager, People Ops Lead
- **Triggers:** rapid headcount growth or reduction, new CHRO hire, M&A requiring HR system consolidation, return-to-office or geographic expansion changes
- **Opening angle:** reference the specific workforce change and the administrative burden it creates

## Marketing Tech (e.g. Adobe Experience Cloud, Braze, Segment)
- **Economic Buyer:** CMO, VP Marketing Operations
- **Champion:** Marketing Ops Manager, Lifecycle/CRM Marketing Lead
- **Triggers:** new CMO hire, brand relaunch, D2C channel expansion, privacy/cookie-deprecation pressure
- **Opening angle:** connect to the stated brand or channel initiative and the personalization/data gap it exposes

## Collaboration / Productivity (e.g. Slack, Notion, Microsoft 365)
- **Economic Buyer:** CIO, VP IT
- **Champion:** IT Ops Manager, Digital Workplace Lead
- **Triggers:** M&A requiring tool consolidation, hybrid-work policy changes, major reorg spanning multiple business units
- **Opening angle:** anchor to the consolidation or hybrid-work friction created by the recent org change

## ERP / Finance Systems (e.g. SAP, Oracle, NetSuite)
- **Economic Buyer:** CFO, COO
- **Champion:** Finance Systems Manager, Controller
- **Triggers:** IPO or acquisition requiring audit-grade systems, ERP vendor end-of-life/support sunset, multi-entity expansion
- **Opening angle:** tie to the compliance or scale event forcing a systems decision on a deadline

## Fallback: platform not listed above

Reason it out in three steps:
1. **What business function does this platform primarily serve?** (revenue, engineering, finance, people, security, marketing, etc.) — that function's VP/C-level is almost always the Economic Buyer.
2. **Who uses it hands-on day to day?** — that's your Champion; usually a manager-level practitioner, not an executive.
3. **What kind of event would make this org need it more urgently than they did six months ago?** — growth, a public incident, a leadership change, or a regulatory shift are the four most reliable trigger categories. Match Section 3/4's research findings against these four to find the real trigger, rather than inventing one.
