## 1. Customer Information

- **Overview:** Marriott International is the world's largest hotel company by property count, operating roughly 9,000 properties across 30+ brands (Marriott Bonvoy, The Ritz-Carlton, W Hotels, Sheraton, St. Regis, The Luxury Collection, and others) in 141 countries and territories. It runs an asset-light franchise/management model — Marriott owns very few hotels itself and instead earns franchise fees, management fees, and cost-reimbursement revenue from independent hotel owners, supplemented by its Marriott Bonvoy loyalty and co-branded credit card business.
- **Headquarters:** Bethesda, Maryland, USA
- **Employees:** Approximately 414,000 associates under Marriott's management as of December 31, 2025 — roughly 148,000 employed directly by Marriott (properties, customer engagement centers, above-property roles) and roughly 266,000 employed by hotel owners but managed by Marriott (source: Marriott International FY2025 Form 10-K, filed 2026).
- **Annual revenue / market cap:** FY2025 total revenues of $26.186 billion, up 4.3% year-over-year, with operating income of $4.141 billion, up 9.9% (source: FY2025 Form 10-K). Q2 2026 revenue was $7.07 billion with Adjusted EBITDA of $1.592 billion, up 13% year-over-year (source: Marriott Q2 2026 earnings release, PRNewswire, Aug 3, 2026). NASDAQ: MAR, trading around $355-356 as of mid-August 2026, up roughly 34% over the trailing 12 months; consensus analyst rating is "Moderate Buy" with an average price target in the high-$380s (source: StockAnalysis.com, MarketBeat).
- **Ownership:** Public — NASDAQ: MAR
- **Key facts:** Marriott Bonvoy surpassed 295 million loyalty members in Q2 2026, after adding 43 million members in 2025 alone, fueled partly by new long-term co-branded credit card agreements with JPMorgan Chase and American Express (source: Hotel Dive, CX Dive, Q2 2026 earnings coverage). The company completed a $355 million acquisition of the citizenM lifestyle-hotel brand in Q3 2025 and raised its full-year 2026 global RevPAR guidance to 3%-3.5% growth after a strong Q2. Marriott is mid-way through a multiyear, roughly $1 billion technology replatforming effort touching its central reservation system, property management system, and loyalty platform — with $1.1 billion in total investment spending planned for 2026, more than a third of it toward digital and technology transformation.

## 2. Funding & Financial Position

- **Public company financials:** FY2025 revenue $26.186B (+4.3%); Q2 2026 revenue $7.07B, Adjusted EBITDA $1.592B (+13% YoY); global RevPAR +3.4% in Q2 2026, with US & Canada RevPAR +5% — the highest quarterly increase in 13 quarters. Q2 total gross fee revenues rose 13% YoY to $1.58B. Stock has climbed roughly 34% over the past 12 months to the mid-$350s, with a consensus "Moderate Buy" rating (source: Marriott Q2 2026 results, PRNewswire and GuruFocus, Aug 2026; StockAnalysis.com; MarketBeat).
- **M&A activity:** Completed the $355 million acquisition of the citizenM lifestyle brand (37 hotels, ~8,800 rooms) in July 2025, with earn-out payments of up to $110 million tied to future brand growth (source: PRNewswire, CoStar, April-July 2025). Marriott also entered a long-term brand/franchise agreement with Sonder Holdings, extending Marriott's distribution reach into Sonder's apartment-style inventory.
- **Financial signals relevant to a purchase decision:** Marriott is in a **selective growth-and-modernization mode**, not a pure cost-cutting posture — RevPAR and fee revenue are both growing and guidance was raised — but it is simultaneously running significant G&A discipline: a January 2025 restructuring cut roughly 833 corporate and regional positions, targeting $80-90 million in annual pre-tax G&A savings starting in 2025, followed later in 2025 by additional reductions in the customer engagement center workforce. That combination — real growth funding available, paired with active headcount/cost discipline in the corporate and service-delivery functions — means a Salesforce expansion pitch lands best when it's framed around efficiency and AI-driven service automation, not "add more seats." Budget planning for 2027 initiatives would typically follow Marriott's Q3 earnings call (historically early November) and its investor-facing commentary at events like the J.P. Morgan Gaming, Lodging, Restaurant & Leisure Management Access Forum in March.

This reporting relationship is **inferred, not independently confirmed**, and is worth validating early in discovery since it materially affects who the real economic buyer is for a platform expansion.

## 5. Tech Stack

- **Currently in use (confirmed or strongly signaled):**
  - **Salesforce Sales Cloud and Service Cloud** — in production since Marriott's 2018 global "customer recognition platform" rollout unifying guest service across 30 brands and 6,500+ properties; actively expanding, per multiple 2026 job postings for Senior Software Engineer (Service Cloud), Salesforce Platform Engineer, and a Director of Salesforce Engineering (source: Salesforce newsroom, April 2018; careers.marriott.com, 2026).
  - **Oracle OPERA Cloud PMS**, running on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI), as part of the core property-management replatform (source: Oracle customer story).
  - **Multicloud infrastructure** spanning AWS (Marriott's data platform migrated to AWS for batch/streaming ingestion into object storage), Microsoft Azure, and OCI, with job postings also referencing Google Cloud and AliCloud expertise.
  - **Snowflake**, adopted to modernize Marriott's core data architecture (source: Snowflake customer blog).
  - **Amadeus and Agilisys**, named technology partners in the CRS/loyalty-platform replacement alongside Oracle (source: Business Travel News/PhocusWire, 2026).
  - **Microsoft 365 Copilot**, piloted with ~100 associates and rolling out to thousands more for meeting summarization and other productivity use cases (source: CIO Dive, 2026).
  - **Harness**, referenced in an AWS re:Invent 2025 session on Marriott's CI/CD and digital-transformation engineering practices.
- **Adjacent/competitive to what we sell:** Microsoft (365 Copilot for productivity AI, and Azure as one of three cloud providers) and Oracle (OPERA PMS plus broader enterprise systems) both have expanding footprints that overlap with where Salesforce would want to extend — particularly around AI/agent tooling and unified customer data. Snowflake's role as Marriott's data-modernization platform is also worth watching: it's complementary to Salesforce Data Cloud today, but any push toward Snowflake-native customer 360 tooling would compete directly with a Data Cloud expansion.
- **Whitespace / gaps:** Marriott is simultaneously (1) replacing its core reservation/PMS/loyalty stack, (2) building a homegrown "agentic mesh" for reusable AI, and (3) running its existing Salesforce Service/Sales Cloud footprint through active engineering investment — but no public evidence confirms Salesforce Data Cloud, Agentforce, or Marketing Cloud are yet part of that picture. Given the 295M-member Bonvoy base and Marriott's own description of wanting a unified, AI-ready guest data layer, that is the clearest whitespace: extending the existing Salesforce footprint into unified customer data and agentic service automation, positioned as complementary to (not a replacement for) the CRS/PMS replatform already underway.

## 6. Entry Points & Persona to Pitch

- **Primary persona to target:** Per the CRM / Sales Engagement playbook category, the Economic Buyer would typically be a CRO, VP Sales Ops, or CMO — but Marriott's actual org structure narrows this: **Drew Pinto, EVP Chief Revenue & Technology Officer**, is the most credible real economic buyer, since his remit combines revenue, digital commerce, IT, and AI in a single budget line (Section 3/4). Global CIO **Naveen Manga** is a critical technical co-sponsor and gatekeeper for anything AI/platform-architecture related.
- **Likely champion profile:** A Salesforce Platform Engineering practitioner or manager inside Marriott's technology org — the open "FLEX Director – Salesforce Engineering" requisition (Section 4) confirms this function exists today at a meaningful scale ("one of the largest Salesforce ecosystems") and is actively growing.
- **Entry point / trigger event:** Two converging triggers make this genuinely timely rather than generic: (1) Naveen Manga has publicly framed 2026 as the year Marriott moves AI "from pilot to scale," and (2) the CRS/PMS/loyalty replatform (Amadeus, Agilisys, Oracle) is moving into hotel-level deployment this year, which means Marriott's guest-data and service layers are being actively re-architected right now — a natural moment to position Salesforce Data Cloud and Agentforce as the unifying layer across the new reservation/loyalty stack and the existing Sales/Service Cloud footprint, rather than trying to open a conversation cold.
- **Recommended opening angle:** Don't pitch Salesforce as something new to adopt — Marriott already runs a large, actively-staffed Salesforce Service and Sales Cloud environment. Open instead around the specific tension Marriott itself has described publicly: it's building a proprietary "agentic mesh" for reusable AI while simultaneously replacing its core reservation/PMS/loyalty systems — ask how guest data will stay unified and AI-actionable across that transition, and where Salesforce's existing footprint fits into (or could accelerate) that architecture.

## 7. Account Penetration & Expansion Strategy

- **Initial wedge (penetration):** Given Salesforce is already embedded (Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, active engineering hiring), the wedge isn't a net-new sale — it's a **scoped Data Cloud pilot** unifying guest/loyalty data from the in-flight CRS/PMS/loyalty replatform with the existing Service Cloud contact-center environment, targeted at the customer engagement center function that absorbed 2025's headcount reductions (Section 2) and could show a fast, defensible efficiency case.
- **Land motion:** A proof-of-concept co-sponsored by the Salesforce Platform Engineering team (champion) and a delegate from Drew Pinto's technology organization, explicitly scoped to one function (contact center / guest service) rather than an enterprise-wide rollout — consistent with Marriott's own "agentic mesh" philosophy of building reusable capability in narrow slices before scaling.
- **Expansion path:** If the Data Cloud/contact-center pilot lands, the next surfaces are (1) Agentforce-driven service automation extending into Bonvoy loyalty servicing at 295M-member scale, and (2) Marketing Cloud/personalization tied to Peggy Fang Roe's Customer Experience, Loyalty & New Ventures organization (Section 3) — both natural adjacencies to the existing Sales/Service Cloud relationship rather than new stakeholder territory.
- **Expansion triggers:** Watch for (1) the CRS/PMS/loyalty replatform reaching "meaningful" hotel-level deployment (executives described 2026 as the deployment year); (2) the CISO seat being filled, which will likely trigger a fresh security/architecture review of AI-adjacent data platforms; (3) Marriott's Q3 2026 earnings call and any FY2027 technology-investment guidance; (4) the outcome of the March 2026 continent leadership realignment settling into steady state, which typically reopens regional commercial budget conversations.

## 9. Discovery Question Guide (First Stakeholder Meeting)

The MEDDPICC Snapshot below marks **Decision Process, Paper Process, Decision Criteria, and the true Economic Buyer/reporting-line question** as inferred or unconfirmed — those are the real gaps worth spending face time on, not re-confirming things research already answered (e.g., don't ask whether Marriott uses Salesforce — we know it does).

- "I saw the CRS, PMS, and loyalty replatform is moving into deployment this year — as that data gets re-architected, how are you thinking about keeping the guest view unified across the new stack and your existing Salesforce environment?" — closes: **Identify Pain** (opener grounded in the actual trigger event from Section 6)

- "What's the cost of not having a unified, AI-ready guest data layer by the time the new reservation and loyalty systems are fully live?" — closes: **Metrics**

- "Who besides Drew Pinto's team would need to be convinced for a Data Cloud or Agentforce expansion to move forward — does Naveen Manga's team weigh in separately?" — closes: **Economic Buyer / Decision Process**

## MEDDPICC Snapshot

- **Metrics:** Not publicly quantified for a Salesforce expansion specifically. Plausible target metrics, inferred from public commentary: reduced customer-engagement-center cost-to-serve (following 2025 headcount reductions in that function), faster/more consistent guest personalization at 295M-member Bonvoy scale, and measurable progress on Naveen Manga's stated 2026 goal of moving AI "from pilot to scale." **Inferred — confirm actual KPI ownership in discovery.**
- **Economic Buyer:** Drew Pinto, EVP Chief Revenue & Technology Officer — name and title confirmed publicly, and his remit (revenue, IT, data, AI) makes him the most credible budget owner. **His direct sign-off authority over a Salesforce-specific expansion is inferred, not confirmed** — Global CIO Naveen Manga is a likely co-approver on anything touching core platform architecture.
- **Decision Criteria:** No public statement found on how Marriott evaluates CRM/data-platform vendors specifically. **Inferred** from context: given the in-flight CRS/PMS/loyalty replatform (Amadeus, Agilisys, Oracle) and the stated "agentic mesh" philosophy, expect Marriott to weight interoperability with existing systems and reusability across functions heavily, over standalone feature breadth.
- **Decision Process:** No public data found. **Inferred/best-estimate** — a public company of Marriott's scale, recently reshuffled at the continent-leadership level, very likely means a multi-stakeholder approval path spanning Pinto's technology organization, regional Chief Commercial Officers, and procurement/security.
- **Paper Process:** **Inferred as heavier than average.** Marriott is operating under a 20-year FTC consent order (from an October 2024 settlement tied to the Starwood-era data breaches affecting over 339 million consumers) plus a separate $52 million settlement with 49 state attorneys general, and currently has an open CISO requisition. Any new platform capability touching guest/loyalty data should expect substantial security and privacy review.
- **Identify Pain:** Marriott is simultaneously replacing its core reservation/PMS/loyalty systems, building proprietary agentic AI infrastructure, and absorbing headcount reductions in customer-facing service functions — a strong, publicly evidenced case for pain around guest-data fragmentation and service-automation capacity during a period of major systems change. **Confirmed pattern from public sources; the specific pain point a Salesforce pitch would target (Data Cloud unification vs. Agentforce automation) is still inferred and should be tested in discovery.**
- **Champion:** A Salesforce Platform Engineering manager or the Director-level role Marriott is actively hiring for (Section 4) — confirmed to exist and be actively growing via job postings; specific individual not named in public sources.
- **Competition:** Internally, Marriott's own "agentic mesh" build-vs-buy posture is the most direct competitive dynamic — any Agentforce pitch is competing with an internal engineering roadmap, not just other vendors. Externally, Microsoft (365 Copilot, Azure) and Oracle (OPERA PMS and adjacent enterprise systems) both have expanding footprints that overlap with where a Salesforce expansion would want to go.
