# Live Roleplay — Transcript Excerpt (Delta Air Lines / Snowflake)

*This is a shortened excerpt of a Step 3 live session, run against the battlecard above, included here to show what the tool produces — not the full session. The full scorecard from this session follows in `delta-snowflake-objection-sparring-scorecard.md`.*

**VP (in character):** Look, I appreciate you coming in, but I'll be straight with you — we tried something like this two years ago. It went over budget and never really delivered what was promised. So I'm not exactly starting from a place of enthusiasm here.

**Seller:** That's completely fair. Can I ask what actually went wrong — was it the platform itself, the migration, or more how success got measured at the end?

**VP:** Honestly, a bit of both. The migration dragged on for almost a year longer than planned, and by the time it was "done," nobody could agree if it had actually hit the goals we set at the start.

**Seller:** That's really useful to know, because that's a project management and success-criteria problem as much as a platform one. Before we go further — what would "done and successful" need to look like this time, in a way everyone would actually agree on?

*(Seller used negative reverse selling correctly here — agreed with the concern instead of arguing past it, and it opened up real detail.)*

**VP:** Fair question. I'd want a clear timeline with checkpoints, and I'd want the win defined before we start, not argued about after.

**Seller:** Understood. We'd build that into the plan from day one, checkpoints included.

**VP:** Okay. Separately — how is this actually different from what we already have, or from Databricks, which I know we're also looking at?

**Seller:** We've got better performance benchmarks and a cleaner UI, honestly, so I think you'd like it more once you see it.

*(Weak response — feature-level defense, no engagement with the VP's actual cost.)*

**VP:** Sure, everyone says that. What I actually want to know is what it's going to save my team, not whether it looks nicer.

*(Escalating — the vague answer didn't land, and the VP is pressing harder, testing whether the seller can recover.)*

**Seller:** Fair pushback. Let me ask instead — what's your data engineering team spending the most hours firefighting on right now? That's usually where the real gap between platforms actually shows up, more than a feature list.

**VP:** ...Honestly, probably pipeline maintenance. We've got two people who spend a good chunk of their week just keeping things from breaking.

**Seller:** That's exactly the kind of cost that doesn't show up in a feature comparison but shows up on a budget line. If we can get real numbers on those hours, that's a much stronger case than anything I could tell you about the UI.

*(Recovered well — pivoted to Command of the Message after the miss, and it landed.)*
